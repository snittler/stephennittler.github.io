Consulting

Energy policy and AI-powered intelligence tools for infrastructure.

I spent a decade inside energy policy: Chief of Staff in the Missouri Senate, four years as a registered lobbyist on energy and tech, and three years managing government affairs and community relations for a multi-state HVDC transmission project with a $2M+ annual public affairs budget.

In 2025, I started building AI tools to solve problems I understood from the inside. Regulatory monitoring that would have taken a team hours per week. Opposition tracking that flags risk before it becomes a headline. Daily intelligence pipelines that do the research work automatically.

Those tools now run in production. They power The Daily Mine, a daily energy newsletter, and a live opposition database covering 78 scored data center and transmission projects across the US.
