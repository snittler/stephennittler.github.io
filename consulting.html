The 7 Root Causes of Project Failure

  1. Late engagement — Communities learn about projects from opponents first
  2. Benefits disconnect — Local costs, distant benefits
  3. Trust deficit — Developer credibility not established early
  4. Information vacuum — Opponents fill the void with misinformation
  5. Process blindness — Underestimating regulatory/political complexity
  6. Coalition gaps — No local champions to counter opposition
  7. Escalation failures — Problems fester until they're crises

This research shapes every engagement — I know what kills projects before the first shovel hits the ground.
